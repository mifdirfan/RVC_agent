## 5. Gaps

- **T13, T14, T15, T18, T19, T20 (C15, turn ends with the front still blocked).** With a static map and adjacent-cell sensors, the front cell after a turn is the side cell that was sensed free when the turn was chosen (C18), so the front is always clear when a turn ends. These rows need a changing environment. They are verified by the unit tests `Controller.T13_*` ... `Controller.T20_*` (`artifacts/TDD/test_execution_results.md`).
- **R6, R7** are scope statements; no scenario tests them directly.
